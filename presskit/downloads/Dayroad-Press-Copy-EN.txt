ONE LINE
An iPhone focus timer that turns study, reading, and work into pixel-art drives, with Stamps to spend on a Home of your own.

SHORT INTRODUCTION
Dayroad is an iPhone focus timer for making room to study, read, or work. Choose a duration and set off: a car travels through a pixel city while you stay with your task. Accumulated focus earns Stamps that you spend yourself to grow your Home.

PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
For moments when getting started feels difficult, another app pulls attention away, or reading and work need time of their own. Dayroad is an iPhone focus timer built around setting aside one session for the task at hand.

Open the drive menu from Home, choose a duration, and set off. A car travels through a pixel city automatically while the timer runs, leaving the user free to study, read, or work without steering. Optional Focus Lock blocks selected apps and categories with Screen Time permission. Website blocking is not supported in version 1.0.

After focusing, take a cafe break. Every 25 accumulated minutes of focus earns one Stamp. Save the number required for the next Home stage, then choose to spend them to unlock it. Home grows through 35 stages; upgrades are not automatic.

Side-on streets and a top-down driving view, clear days and rainy evenings, music and cafe breaks set the rhythm of focusing and unwinding. Visible Home progress is designed to give the next focus session a small goal to look forward to.
