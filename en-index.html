05 / BEFORE YOU GO

Before you set off

Can I download Dayroad now?+

Yes. Dayroad is available now on the App Store. Use the download buttons on this page to install it on your iPhone or iPad from the official App Store.

Will I be charged when the free trial ends?+

No. The Focus Drive Pass is a one-time purchase you choose to make. Free Drive stays free after the trial ends.

How do Stamps work?+

Stamps are earned from accumulated focus time and used to unlock Home upgrades. Free Drive does not award focus rewards.

What happens to my purchase and progress on a new device?+

Restore your pass using the Apple Account you purchased with. Restore records, Stamps, and Home progress separately through private iCloud sync or a JSON backup. Back up your existing records first.

Which devices and languages are supported?+

Dayroad supports iPhone and iPad in Korean, Japanese, and English. Features such as Focus Lock and Live Activities require a supported device and system permissions.
